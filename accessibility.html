Accessibility

We aim to meet the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and WCAG 2.0 Level AA for this public site.

This speed radar toolkit is a volunteer community site. Public pages (results, how-to guide, and this statement) do not require a login. The optional password on the logging form is only a soft gate for event volunteers.

What we support

  • Skip link to main content on every page
  • Keyboard access to navigation, forms, and street chart tabs
  • Visible focus indicators on interactive controls
  • Form labels and live error messages on the tracker
  • Text summaries beside charts on the results page
  • Semantic landmarks, headings, and table captions where used

Known limits

  • Results charts are drawn with Chart.js; the visual canvas is decorative for screen readers when a text summary is present. Prefer the summaries and the “By street” table for the same figures.
  • Published event data depends on volunteer CSV uploads; incomplete exports may limit what the results page can show.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier using this site, contact your Strong Towns Niagara Falls chapter organizers. Describe the page, the assistive technology you use (if any), and what you were trying to do.
